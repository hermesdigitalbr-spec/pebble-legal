Privacy Policy

Pebble: Pregnancy Day by Day · Effective November 15, 2026

Overview

Pebble is developed by Hermes Inteligência Digital Ltda ("we"). This policy explains how Pebble handles information.

In short: Pebble does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Everything you add — your due date, names, bump and baby photos, journal entries, appointments, kicks, contractions, weight, checklists, milestones and growth data — is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it.

Photos you take or import are saved inside the app on your device. Pebble asks for camera and photo access only when you use those features.

Backups

Your iPhone may include Pebble data in your own iCloud or computer backup, under your Apple account and Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to these backups.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Health information

Pebble is for information and keepsakes. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical care. Always talk to your doctor or midwife about your health and your baby's.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from Me › Delete everything in the app, or by deleting the app.

Children

Pebble is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
